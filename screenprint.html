Our Shared Canvas: Quarters 2 & 3 Project

Project Overview and What I Learned

In this New Media and Technology for Social Change class, students created screen prints to educate people about a problem of their choosing. The print had to include a picture of themselves, and also was required to contain text. The issue that I chose to address was political corruption. To create this print, we had to make a photoshop document with this picture depicting some sort of artistic message along with a photo of us. We used WACOM tablets to assist us in this endeavor, along with Loopy models to map out our issues. Additionally, we had to design the frames we were to use in the screenprinting process. We used Adobe Illustrator, specifically the drawing and dimensioning tools to outline the frames. To construct the frames, we started by measuring the wood. We used carpenter’s squares to measure the angle and length of the wooden segments that make up the frame. Then, we cut the wood using high-powered saws. Soon after, we glued the wooden pieces together to give us a base structure of the frame. We then stapled the frame together to finish the construction of the base frame. Later, we would add the mesh to the frame by stapling that on as well. The stapling was a two person job, because one person had to hold the mesh while the other operated the staple gun. Next, we cut out the print in vinyl using Silhouette machines, and then ironed the cut-out design onto the frame. To prepare to print, we mixed our paints and affixed our frames to hinges on the table. We printed on three sheets of paper, a box, and a piece of fabric that were later sewn together, hence "our shared canvas". Throughout the project, we captured video of the seperate steps and eventually used video editing software to create a short documentary of the process. During this project, I learned how to use photoshop, cut something out using a Silhouette machine, and use video editing software.

Another second-biggest-heading

The 21st century skill that I improved in the most in was communtcation and collaboration. Throughout the project, I learned how to communicate major issues using real data, and creative ways to draw attention to these problems using digital design. An ability to use all of these modern technologies allows me to create impactful art projects, and possibly give me experience I could use in a future career.
