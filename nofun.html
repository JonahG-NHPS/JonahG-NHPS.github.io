Biotechnology Breakthroughs in Genetics: Quarter 4 Project

Project Overview and What I Learned

During this project, we tested our DNA using several machines to answer questions about who we would match with and whether race is biologically real. We started the experiment by swabbing our cheeks to extract DNA. We then transferred the DNA to a tube with extraction buffer already in it. Next, we incubated the tubes for ten minutes at 95 degrees Celsius. After that, we added several PCR reagents to an empty tube, along with our DNA sample. These tubes were then placed in the miniPCR machine, where the machine cycled through extreme heat to prepare the mixture for gel eletrophoresis. To start the gel electrophoresis process, we poured the liquid agarose mixture into a mold with two Electrodams and a comb to create the rectangulat holes. Once the gel solidified, we were ready to run the gel. We added the electrophoresis buffer to the container with the gel, and then inserted the DNA into the wells using micropipettes. We then ran the gel, and got our results. Over the course of this process, I learned many things, such as what the process of gel electrophoresis is, how it works, and why it is useful. I also learned what a miniPCR machine is and how to use one, as wel as how to use a micropipette.

So... Is Race Biologically Real?

Race is not biologically real. As can be seen in the data of the class of 24’-25’, two people who identify as Hispanic/Latino have first bands of 447 and 671 with full confidence. This is such extreme variation that every race has at least one DNA sample in between these. One version of the idea of race is that (1) human beings are distinctive from other humans based on what area of the world their ancestors adapted in, and therefore are similar to people from that area in terms of biological structures than they are humans from a different area and that (2) you can tell which of these regional types a person is by simple visible phenotypical traits such as hair texture and color, skin color, amongst others. In 2024-25, there were 61 matches people had with someone of the same race and 251 matches between people of different races. This, combined with the variation in band size, proves that biological race is purely a myth.
